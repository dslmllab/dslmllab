## 🎯 About Us

The **Decentralized Science Lab (dSL)** at Kennesaw State University is a cutting-edge research group performing innovative research into decentralized computing concepts and their enabling technologies. Led by **Dr. Reza M. Parizi**, we provide R&D solutions in multidisciplinary domains.

<div align="center">

| 🧠 **AI Security** | 🔐 **Web3 Security** | ⛓️ **Blockchain Systems** | 🤖 **AI Agents** |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| Securing AI systems and using AI for cybersecurity | Smart contract auditing and DeFi security | Scalable and secure blockchain architectures | Autonomous systems and federated learning |

</div>
