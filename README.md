## About

The Decentralized Science Lab (dSL) is a research group at Kennesaw State University conducting research into decentralized computing concepts and their enabling technologies. The lab is directed by Dr. Reza M. Parizi and works across several multidisciplinary domains.

## Research Areas

- **AI Security** — Securing AI systems and applying AI to cybersecurity problems.
- **Web3 Security** — Smart contract auditing and DeFi security.
- **Blockchain Systems** — Scalable and secure blockchain architectures.
- **AI Agents and Federated Learning** — Autonomous systems and distributed learning.

## Featured Projects

### AI and Large Language Models

- **[LLMSmartSec](https://github.com/dslmllab/LLMSmartSec)** — Security analysis for smart contracts using large language models.
- **[Automated Judging of LLM-based Smart Contract Security Auditors](https://github.com/dslmllab/Automated-Judging-of-LLM-based-Smart-Contract-Security-Auditors)** — Automated evaluation framework for LLM-based security auditors.

### Blockchain and DeFi Security

- **[An AI Multi-Model Approach to DeFi Project Trust Scoring and Security](https://github.com/dslmllab/An-AI-Multi-Model-Approach-to-DeFi-Project-Trust-Scoring-and-Security)** — Multi-model trust scoring for DeFi projects.
- **[Hunting DeFi Scams](https://github.com/dslmllab/Hunting-DeFi-Scams)** — Research and tooling for identifying DeFi scams.

### Federated Learning and AI Agents

- **[AgentFL](https://github.com/dslmllab/AgentFL-AI-Orchestrated-Agents-for-Federated-Learning)** — AI-orchestrated agents for federated learning.
- **[dSL Lab Coding Challenge](https://github.com/dslmllab/dSL-Lab-Coding-Challenge)** — Coding challenges for prospective researchers.
